About Bonnie

A Trusted Partner Through Life's Most Challenging Moments

Navigating the complexities of caregiving and aging can be overwhelming, often leading to emotional and physical strain. As a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with over 30 years of experience in healthcare, I provide compassionate support and practical guidance for individuals and families facing these challenges.

I specialize in helping those coping with chronic or terminal illness, supporting caregivers of elderly loved ones, and addressing the family conflicts that can arise during these difficult times. My approach focuses on understanding, empathy, and empowering you to find balance, healing, and peace amidst life's transitions.

I have extensive experience supporting older adults as they navigate grief related to the loss of loved ones, independence, and the challenges of aging. As a Certified Dementia Practitioner with a strong background in grief counseling, I offer compassionate guidance and support to both individuals and caregivers.
